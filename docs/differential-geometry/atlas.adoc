== Differential geometry

=== Constructing an atlas
An atlas is a collection of charts that provide local coordinate systems. A chart is a map that supplies a local coordinate system. An atlas plays an important role in understanding the structure of surfaces and manifolds.

.Examples
* <<atlas_s1_transition.adoc#,Atlas of the unit circle S^1^ (the real 1-dimensional projective line)>>
* <<atlas_s2_angular_coords.adoc#,Atlas of the unit sphere S^2^ (angular coordinates)>>
* <<atlas_s2_stereographic.adoc#,Atlas of the unit sphere S^2^ (stereographic projection)>>
* <<atlas_rp1.adoc#,Atlas of the real projective line RP^1^>>
