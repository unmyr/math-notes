== 2形式の外微分: 座標に依存しない定義と座標表示の一致

This note verifies that the coordinate expression of the exterior derivative
for a 2-form agrees with the coordinate-free definition (see equation <<../books/微分幾何入門/chap02_2_5.adoc#mjx-eqn-def_2_13,定義(2.13)>>).


=== 例2-1. 2-形式の場合 (解法1)

==== 例2-1. Step1 局所座標による定義による導出を行う

式 <<../books/微分幾何入門/chap02_2_5.adoc#mjx-eqn-def_2_13,定義(2.13)>>の左辺を局所座標を用いて書く。

[stem,latexmath]
++++
\begin{align}
d\omega
&= \frac{1}{2!}\left(\partial_{\mu}\omega_{\nu_{1}\nu_{2}}\right) \, dx^{\mu} \wedge dx^{\nu_{1}} \wedge dx^{\nu_{2}} \\
&=
\frac{1}{2!}
\biggl\{
  \frac{1}{6}\left(\partial_{\mu}\omega_{\nu_{1}\nu_{2}}\right) \, dx^{\mu} \wedge dx^{\nu_{1}} \wedge dx^{\nu_{2}}
+ \frac{1}{6}\left(\partial_{\mu}\omega_{\nu_{1}\nu_{2}}\right) \, dx^{\mu} \wedge dx^{\nu_{1}} \wedge dx^{\nu_{2}} \\
&\quad
+ \frac{1}{6}\left(\partial_{\mu}\omega_{\nu_{1}\nu_{2}}\right) \, dx^{\mu} \wedge dx^{\nu_{1}} \wedge dx^{\nu_{2}}
+ \frac{1}{6}\left(\partial_{\mu}\omega_{\nu_{1}\nu_{2}}\right) \, dx^{\mu} \wedge dx^{\nu_{1}} \wedge dx^{\nu_{2}} \\
&\quad
+ \frac{1}{6}\left(\partial_{\mu}\omega_{\nu_{1}\nu_{2}}\right) \, dx^{\mu} \wedge dx^{\nu_{1}} \wedge dx^{\nu_{2}}
+ \frac{1}{6}\left(\partial_{\mu}\omega_{\nu_{1}\nu_{2}}\right) \, dx^{\mu} \wedge dx^{\nu_{1}} \wedge dx^{\nu_{2}}
\biggr\} \\
&=
\frac{1}{2! \cdot 6}
\bigl\{
   \left(\partial_{\mu}\omega_{\nu_{1}\nu_{2}}\right) \, dx^{\mu} \wedge dx^{\nu_{1}} \wedge dx^{\nu_{2}}
+ \left(\partial_{\nu_{1}}\omega_{\mu\nu_{2}}\right) \, dx^{\nu_{1}} \wedge dx^{\mu} \wedge dx^{\nu_{2}} \\
&\quad\quad\quad
+ \left(\partial_{\nu_{1}}\omega_{\mu\nu_{2}}\right) \, dx^{\nu_{1}} \wedge dx^{\mu} \wedge dx^{\nu_{2}}
+ \left(\partial_{\nu_{2}}\omega_{\mu\nu_{1}}\right) \, dx^{\nu_{2}} \wedge dx^{\mu} \wedge dx^{\nu_{1}} \\
&\quad\quad\quad
+ \left(\partial_{\nu_{2}}\omega_{\mu\nu_{1}}\right) \, dx^{\nu_{2}} \wedge dx^{\mu} \wedge dx^{\nu_{1}}
+ \left(\partial_{\mu}\omega_{\nu_{2}\nu_{1}}\right) \, dx^{\mu} \wedge dx^{\nu_{2}} \wedge dx^{\nu_{1}}
\bigr\} \tag{dω#1} \label{ex_2_2_local_d_omega_1} \\
&=
\frac{1}{2! \cdot 6}
\bigl\{
   \left(\partial_{\mu}\omega_{\nu_{1}\nu_{2}}\right) \, dx^{\mu} \wedge dx^{\nu_{1}} \wedge dx^{\nu_{2}}
- \left(\partial_{\nu_{1}}\omega_{\mu\nu_{2}}\right) \, dx^{\mu} \wedge dx^{\nu_{1}} \wedge dx^{\nu_{2}} \\
&\quad\quad\quad
- \left(\partial_{\nu_{1}}\omega_{\mu\nu_{2}}\right) \, dx^{\mu} \wedge dx^{\nu_{1}} \wedge dx^{\nu_{2}}
+ \left(\partial_{\nu_{2}}\omega_{\mu\nu_{1}}\right) \, dx^{\mu} \wedge dx^{\nu_{1}} \wedge dx^{\nu_{2}} \\
&\quad\quad\quad
+ \left(\partial_{\nu_{2}}\omega_{\mu\nu_{1}}\right) \, dx^{\mu} \wedge dx^{\nu_{1}} \wedge dx^{\nu_{2}}
- \left(\partial_{\mu}\omega_{\nu_{2}\nu_{1}}\right) \, dx^{\mu} \wedge dx^{\nu_{1}} \wedge dx^{\nu_{2}}
\bigr\} \tag{dω#2} \label{ex_2_2_local_d_omega_2} \\
&=
\frac{1}{2! \cdot 6}
\left\{
   2 \left(\partial_{\mu}\omega_{\nu_{1}\nu_{2}}\right)
- 2 \left(\partial_{\nu_{1}}\omega_{\mu\nu_{2}}\right)
+ 2 \left(\partial_{\nu_{2}}\omega_{\mu\nu_{1}}\right) \right\}
\, dx^{\mu} \wedge dx^{\nu_{1}} \wedge dx^{\nu_{2}} \\
&=
\frac{1}{6}
\left(
   \partial_{\mu}\omega_{\nu_{1}\nu_{2}}
- \partial_{\nu_{1}}\omega_{\mu\nu_{2}}
+ \partial_{\nu_{2}}\omega_{\mu\nu_{1}}
\right) \, dx^{\mu} \wedge dx^{\nu_{1}} \wedge dx^{\nu_{2}} \\
\end{align}
++++

なお、 途中の式変形は次のような操作を行っている

* latexmath:[\eqref{ex_2_2_local_d_omega_1}] で latexmath:[(\mu, \nu_{1}, \nu_{1}) \leftrightarrow (\nu_{1}, \mu, \nu_{2})] のように添え字を入れ替えている
* latexmath:[\eqref{ex_2_2_local_d_omega_2}] で latexmath:[dx^{\nu_{1}} \wedge dx^\mu \wedge dx^{\nu_{1}} = - dx^\mu \wedge dx^{\nu_{1}} \wedge dx^{\nu_{1}}] を適用

ここで、latexmath:[A_{\mu\nu_{1}\nu_{2}}] を次のように定義する。

[stem,latexmath]
++++
A_{\mu\nu_{1}\nu_{2}} := 
 \partial_{\mu}\omega_{\nu_{1}\nu_{2}}
- \partial_{\nu_{1}}\omega_{\mu\nu_{2}}
+ \partial_{\nu_{2}}\omega_{\mu\nu_{1}} \\
++++

このとき、 latexmath:[A_{\mu\nu_{1}\nu_{2}}] は完全反対称性を持つ。

[stem,latexmath]
++++
\begin{aligned}
A_{\nu_{1}\mu\nu_{2}}
&= \partial_{\nu_{1}}\omega_{\mu\nu_{2}}
- \partial_{\mu}\omega_{\nu_{1}\nu_{2}}
+ \partial_{\nu_{2}}\omega_{\nu_{1}\mu} \\
&= 
- \partial_{\mu}\omega_{\nu_{1}\nu_{2}}
+ \partial_{\nu_{1}}\omega_{\mu\nu_{2}}
+ \partial_{\nu_{2}}\omega_{\nu_{1}\mu} \\
&=
- \partial_{\mu}\omega_{\nu_{1}\nu_{2}}
+ \partial_{\nu_{1}}\omega_{\mu\nu_{2}}
- \partial_{\nu_{2}}\omega_{\mu\nu_{1}} \\
&= -A_{\mu\nu_{1}\nu_{2}} \\
A_{\mu\nu_{2}\nu_{1}}
&= \partial_{\mu}\omega_{\nu_{2}\nu_{1}}
- \partial_{\nu_{2}}\omega_{\mu\nu_{1}}
+ \partial_{\nu_{1}}\omega_{\mu\nu_{2}} \\
&= - \partial_{\mu}\omega_{\nu_{1}\nu_{2}}
+ \partial_{\nu_{1}}\omega_{\mu\nu_{2}}
- \partial_{\nu_{2}}\omega_{\mu\nu_{1}} \\
&= -A_{\mu\nu_{1}\nu_{2}} \\
\end{aligned}
++++

双対ペアリングを多重線形写像に拡張した関数に対して、2次の対称群による偶置換・奇置換の符号による計算は次のようになる。

[stem,latexmath]
++++
\begin{align}
& d\omega(X_1,X_2,X_3) \\
&= \frac{1}{3!} A_{\mu\nu_{1}\nu_{2}} (dx^{\mu} \wedge dx^{\nu_{1}})(X_1,X_2,X_3) \\
&= \frac{1}{3!} A_{\mu\nu_{1}\nu_{2}} \sum_{\sigma \in S_3}\mathrm{sgn}
\begin{pmatrix}
\mu & \nu_{1} & \nu_{2} \\
\sigma(\mu) & \sigma(\nu_{1}) & \sigma(\nu_{2}) \\ 
\end{pmatrix}
dx^{\sigma(\mu)} \otimes dx^{\sigma(\nu_{1})} (X_1,X_2,X_3) \\
&= \frac{1}{3!} A_{\mu\nu_{1}\nu_{2}} \sum_{\sigma \in S_3}\mathrm{sgn}
\begin{pmatrix}
\mu & \nu_{1} & \nu_{2} \\
\sigma(\mu) & \sigma(\nu_{1}) & \sigma(\nu_{2}) \\ 
\end{pmatrix}
X_1^{\sigma(\mu)} X_2^{\sigma(\nu_{1})} X_3^{\sigma(\nu_{2})} \\
&= \frac{1}{3!} \sum_{\sigma \in S_3}\mathrm{sgn}
\begin{pmatrix}
\mu & \nu_{1} & \nu_{2} \\
\sigma(\mu) & \sigma(\nu_{1}) & \sigma(\nu_{2}) \\ 
\end{pmatrix}
A_{\mu\nu_{1}\nu_{2}} X_1^{\sigma(\mu)} X_2^{\sigma(\nu_{1})} X_3^{\sigma(\nu_{2})} \\
&= \frac{1}{3!} \sum_{\sigma \in S_3}\mathrm{sgn}\left(\sigma^{-1}\right)
A_{\sigma(\mu)\sigma(\nu_{1})\sigma(\nu_{2})} X_1^{\mu} X_2^{\nu_{1}} X_3^{\nu_{2}} \\
&= \frac{1}{3!} \sum_{\sigma \in S_3}\mathrm{sgn}\left(\sigma\right)
A_{\sigma(\mu)\sigma(\nu_{1})\sigma(\nu_{2})} X_1^{\mu} X_2^{\nu_{1}} X_3^{\nu_{2}} \\
&= \frac{1}{3!} \sum_{\sigma \in S_3} A_{\mu\nu_{1}\nu_{2}} X_1^{\mu} X_2^{\nu_{1}} X_3^{\nu_{2}} \\
&= \frac{1}{3!} A_{\mu\nu_{1}\nu_{2}} X_1^{\mu} X_2^{\nu_{1}} X_3^{\nu_{2}} \sum_{\sigma \in S_3} 1 \\
&= \frac{1}{3!} A_{\mu\nu_{1}\nu_{2}} X_1^{\mu} X_2^{\nu_{1}} X_3^{\nu_{2}} |S_3| \\
&= A_{\mu\nu_{1}\nu_{2}} X_1^{\mu} X_2^{\nu_{1}} X_3^{\nu_{2}}.
\end{align}
++++

したがって、後の座標非依存の導出で得られる
latexmath:[\left( \partial_{\mu}\omega_{\nu_{1}\nu_{2}}
- \partial_{\nu_{1}}\omega_{\mu\nu_{2}}
+ \partial_{\nu_{2}}\omega_{\mu\nu_{1}}\right) X_1^{\mu} X_2^{\nu_{1}} X_2^{\nu_{2}}] と一致する。

==== 例2-1. Step2. 座標に依存しない定義の展開
まず、2-形式の座標非依存定義を確認する。

[stem,latexmath]
++++
\begin{align}
& d\omega(X_1,X_2,X_3) \\
&= (-1)^{1+1} X_1(\omega(X_2,X_3)) + (-1)^{2+1} X_2(\omega(X_1,X_3)) + (-1)^{3+1} X_3(\omega(X_1,X_2)) \\
&\quad - (-1)^{1+2+1} \omega(\lbrack X_1,X_2 \rbrack, X_3)
            - (-1)^{1+3+1} \omega(\lbrack X_1,X_3 \rbrack, X_2)
            - (-1)^{2+3+1} \omega(\lbrack X_2,X_3 \rbrack, X_1) \\
&= X_1(\omega(X_2,X_3)) - X_2(\omega(X_1,X_3)) + X_3(\omega(X_1,X_2)) \tag{dω(X₁,X₂,X₃)#1} \label{ex_2_1_d_omega_123_1} \\
&\quad - \omega(\lbrack X_1,X_2 \rbrack, X_3) + \omega(\lbrack X_1,X_3 \rbrack, X_2) - \omega(\lbrack X_2,X_3 \rbrack, X_1) \tag{dω(X₁,X₂,X₃)#2} \label{ex_2_1_d_omega_123_2}.
\end{align}
++++

ここでは、まず座標非依存な定義に現れる方向微分項を成分表示に展開する。

この計算のポイントは、まず各項を成分表示に戻し、次に同じ型の項を揃えてから、反対称性 latexmath:[\omega_{\mu\nu}=-\omega_{\nu\mu}] を使って相殺することにある。見落としやすいのは、項の比較ができるように添え字の順序を揃えるところである。

==== 例2-1. Step3 ベクトル場の作用項を展開する

方向微分項 latexmath:[X_i(\omega(X_j,X_k))] を成分表示に展開する。

[stem,latexmath]
++++
\omega(X_j,X_k)=\omega_{\mu\nu}X_j^{\mu}X_k^{\nu},
\qquad \omega_{\mu\nu}=-\omega_{\nu\mu}
++++

と書ける。ベクトル場の作用 latexmath:[X_i(f)=X_i^{\rho}\partial_{\rho}f] であるから、

[stem,latexmath]
++++
\begin{aligned}
X_i(\omega(X_j,X_k))
&=X_i^{\rho}\partial_{\rho} \left(\omega_{\mu\nu}X_j^{\mu}X_k^{\nu}\right) \\
&=X_i^{\rho}\left(\partial_{\rho}\omega_{\mu\nu}\right) X_j^{\mu}X_k^{\nu}
+ X_i^{\rho} \left(
  \omega_{\mu\nu} X_j^{\mu} \left(\partial_{\rho}X_k^{\nu}\right)
+ \omega_{\mu\nu} \left(\partial_{\rho}X_j^{\mu}\right) X_k^{\nu}
\right) \\
&=\left(\partial_{\rho}\omega_{\mu\nu}\right) X_i^{\rho}X_j^{\mu}X_k^{\nu}
+ \left(
   \omega_{\mu\nu} \left(X_i^{\rho} \partial_{\rho}X_j^{\mu}\right) X_k^{\nu}
+ \omega_{\mu\nu} \left (X_i^{\rho} \partial_{\rho}X_k^{\nu}\right) X_j^{\mu}
\right) \\
&=\left(\partial_{\rho}\omega_{\mu\nu}\right) X_i^{\rho}X_j^{\mu}X_k^{\nu}
+ \left(
   \omega_{\mu\nu} \left(X_i^{\rho} \partial_{\rho}X_j^{\mu}\right) X_k^{\nu}
+ \omega_{\nu\mu} \left(X_i^{\rho} \partial_{\rho}X_k^{\mu}\right) X_j^{\nu}
\right) \\
&=\left(\partial_{\rho}\omega_{\mu\nu}\right) X_i^{\rho}X_j^{\mu}X_k^{\nu}
+ \omega_{\mu\nu} \left(
  (X_i^{\rho} \partial_{\rho}X_j^{\mu}) X_k^{\nu}
- (X_i^{\rho} \partial_{\rho}X_k^{\mu}) X_j^{\nu}
\right).
\end{aligned}
++++

これを latexmath:[(i,j,k)=(1,2,3),(2,1,3),(3,1,2)] に適用すると、

[stem,latexmath]
++++
\begin{aligned}
X_1(\omega(X_2,X_3))
&=\left(\partial_{\rho}\omega_{\mu\nu}\right) X_1^{\rho}X_2^{\mu}X_3^{\nu}
+ \omega_{\mu\nu} \left(
  (X_1^{\rho} \partial_{\rho}X_2^{\mu}) X_3^{\nu}
- (X_1^{\rho} \partial_{\rho}X_3^{\mu}) X_2^{\nu}
\right), \\

X_2(\omega(X_1,X_3))
&=\left(\partial_{\rho}\omega_{\mu\nu}\right) X_2^{\rho}X_1^{\mu}X_3^{\nu}
+ \omega_{\mu\nu} \left(
  (X_2^{\rho} \partial_{\rho}X_1^{\mu}) X_3^{\nu}
- (X_2^{\rho} \partial_{\rho}X_3^{\mu}) X_1^{\nu}
\right), \\

X_3(\omega(X_1,X_2))
&=\left(\partial_{\rho}\omega_{\mu\nu}\right) X_3^{\rho}X_1^{\mu}X_2^{\nu}
+ \omega_{\mu\nu} \left(
  (X_3^{\rho} \partial_{\rho}X_1^{\mu}) X_2^{\nu}
- (X_3^{\rho} \partial_{\rho}X_2^{\mu}) X_1^{\nu}
\right). \\
\end{aligned}
++++

==== 例2-1. Step4 添字の入れ替えと整理

外微分の定義に現れる方向微分項 latexmath:[\eqref{ex_2_1_d_omega_123_1}] に代入して式を整理する。

[stem,latexmath]
++++
\begin{aligned}
& X_1(\omega(X_2,X_3)) - X_2(\omega(X_1,X_3)) + X_3(\omega(X_1,X_2)) \\
&=
\left(\partial_{\rho}\omega_{\mu\nu}\right) X_1^{\rho}X_2^{\mu}X_3^{\nu}
+ \omega_{\mu\nu} \left(
  (X_1^{\rho} \partial_{\rho}X_2^{\mu}) X_3^{\nu}
- (X_1^{\rho} \partial_{\rho}X_3^{\mu}) X_2^{\nu}
\right) \\
&\quad
 - \left(\partial_{\rho}\omega_{\mu\nu}\right) X_2^{\rho}X_1^{\mu}X_3^{\nu}
- \omega_{\mu\nu} \left(
  (X_2^{\rho} \partial_{\rho}X_1^{\mu}) X_3^{\nu}
- (X_2^{\rho} \partial_{\rho}X_3^{\mu}) X_1^{\nu}
\right) \\
&\quad
 + \left(\partial_{\rho}\omega_{\mu\nu}\right) X_3^{\rho}X_1^{\mu}X_2^{\nu}
+ \omega_{\mu\nu} \left(
  (X_3^{\rho} \partial_{\rho}X_1^{\mu}) X_2^{\nu}
- (X_3^{\rho} \partial_{\rho}X_2^{\mu}) X_1^{\nu}
\right) \\
&=
\left(\partial_{\rho}\omega_{\mu\nu}\right) X_1^{\rho}X_2^{\mu}X_3^{\nu}
 - \left(\partial_{\rho}\omega_{\mu\nu}\right) X_2^{\rho}X_1^{\mu}X_3^{\nu}
 + \left(\partial_{\rho}\omega_{\mu\nu}\right) X_3^{\rho}X_1^{\mu}X_2^{\nu} \\
&\quad
+ \omega_{\mu\nu} \left(
  (X_1^{\rho} \partial_{\rho}X_2^{\mu}) X_3^{\nu} - (X_1^{\rho} \partial_{\rho}X_3^{\mu}) X_2^{\nu}
\right) \\
&\quad
- \omega_{\mu\nu} \left(
  (X_2^{\rho} \partial_{\rho}X_1^{\mu}) X_3^{\nu} - (X_2^{\rho} \partial_{\rho}X_3^{\mu}) X_1^{\nu}
\right) \\
&\quad
+ \omega_{\mu\nu} \left(
  (X_3^{\rho} \partial_{\rho}X_1^{\mu}) X_2^{\nu} - (X_3^{\rho} \partial_{\rho}X_2^{\mu}) X_1^{\nu}
\right) \\
\end{aligned}
++++

続けて式を整理する。
1,2,3項については、次の添え字の入れ替えを行っている。同じ形の積、例えば latexmath:[X_1^{\rho} X_2^{\mu} X_3^{\nu}]を含む項を比較できるように、添え字の順序を揃える。latexmath:[\rho], latexmath:[\mu], latexmath:[\nu]は、ダミー添字であり、総和 latexmath:[\sum_{\rho,\mu,\nu}]は省略されている。したがって、同じ総和の中で添字を交換しても式の値は変わらず、項をまとめるための書き換えとして扱える。

* 1項: latexmath:[(\rho, \mu, \nu) \leftrightarrow (\rho, \mu, \nu)]
* 2項: latexmath:[(\rho, \mu, \nu) \leftrightarrow (\mu, \rho, \nu)]
* 3項: latexmath:[(\rho, \mu, \nu) \leftrightarrow (\nu, \rho, \mu)]

[stem,latexmath]
++++
\begin{aligned}
&=
\left(
  \partial_{\rho}\omega_{\mu\nu} - \partial_{\mu}\omega_{\rho\nu} + \partial_{\nu}\omega_{\rho\mu}
\right) X_1^{\rho}X_2^{\mu}X_3^{\nu} \\
&\quad
+ \omega_{\mu\nu} \left(
  X_2^{\rho} \left(\partial_{\rho}X_3^{\mu}\right) - X_3^{\rho} \left(\partial_{\rho}X_2^{\mu}\right)
\right) X_1^{\nu} \\
&\quad
- \omega_{\mu\nu} \left(
  X_1^{\rho} \left(\partial_{\rho}X_3^{\mu}\right) - X_3^{\rho} \left(\partial_{\rho}X_1^{\mu}\right)
\right) X_2^{\nu} \\
&\quad
+ \omega_{\mu\nu} \left(
  X_1^{\rho} \left(\partial_{\rho}X_2^{\mu}\right) - X_2^{\rho} \left(\partial_{\rho}X_1^{\mu}\right)
\right) X_3^{\nu} \\
\end{aligned}
++++

続けて、ベクトル場の交換子の成分 latexmath:[\lbrack X_i,X_j \rbrack^{\mu} = X_i^{\rho}\partial_{\rho}X_j^{\mu}  - X_j^{\rho}\partial_{\rho}X_i^{\mu}]を用いて式をまとめる。

[stem,latexmath]
++++
\begin{aligned}
&=
\left(
  \partial_{\rho}\omega_{\mu\nu} - \partial_{\mu}\omega_{\rho\nu} + \partial_{\nu}\omega_{\rho\mu}
\right) X_1^{\rho}X_2^{\mu}X_3^{\nu} \\
&\quad
+ \omega_{\mu\nu} [X_2, X_3]^{\mu} X_1^{\nu}
- \omega_{\mu\nu} [X_1, X_3]^{\mu} X_2^{\nu}
+ \omega_{\mu\nu} [X_1, X_2]^{\mu} X_3^{\nu} \\
&=
\left(
  \partial_{\rho}\omega_{\mu\nu} - \partial_{\mu}\omega_{\rho\nu} + \partial_{\nu}\omega_{\rho\mu}
\right) X_1^{\rho}X_2^{\mu}X_3^{\nu} \\
&\quad
+ \omega \left([X_2, X_3]^{\mu}, X_1\right)
- \omega \left([X_1, X_3]^{\mu}, X_2\right)
+ \omega \left([X_1, X_2]^{\mu}, X_3\right).
\end{aligned}
++++

以上より、外微分の定義に現れる方向微分項の和を次のようにまとめることができた。

[stem,latexmath]
++++
\begin{aligned}
& X_1(\omega(X_2,X_3)) - X_2(\omega(X_1,X_3)) + X_3(\omega(X_1,X_2)) \\
&=
\left(
  \partial_{\rho}\omega_{\mu\nu} - \partial_{\mu}\omega_{\rho\nu} + \partial_{\nu}\omega_{\rho\mu}
\right) X_1^{\rho}X_2^{\mu}X_3^{\nu} \\
&\quad
+ \omega \left([X_2, X_3]^{\mu}, X_1\right)
- \omega \left([X_1, X_3]^{\mu}, X_2\right)
+ \omega \left([X_1, X_2]^{\mu}, X_3\right).
\end{aligned}
++++

==== 例2-1. Step5 交換子項との相殺

[stem,latexmath]
++++
\begin{align}
& d\omega(X_1,X_2,X_3) \\
&= X_1(\omega(X_2,X_3)) - X_2(\omega(X_1,X_3)) + X_3(\omega(X_1,X_2)) \\
&\quad - \omega(\lbrack X_1,X_2 \rbrack, X_3) + \omega(\lbrack X_1,X_3 \rbrack, X_2) - \omega(\lbrack X_2,X_3 \rbrack, X_1) \\
&=
\left(
  \partial_{\rho}\omega_{\mu\nu} - \partial_{\mu}\omega_{\rho\nu} + \partial_{\nu}\omega_{\rho\mu}
\right) X_1^{\rho}X_2^{\mu}X_3^{\nu} \\
&\quad
+ \omega \left([X_2, X_3], X_1\right)
- \omega \left([X_1, X_3], X_2\right)
+ \omega \left([X_1, X_2], X_3\right) \\
&\quad - \omega(\lbrack X_1,X_2 \rbrack, X_3) + \omega(\lbrack X_1,X_3 \rbrack, X_2) - \omega(\lbrack X_2,X_3 \rbrack, X_1) \\
&=
\left(
  \partial_{\rho}\omega_{\mu\nu} - \partial_{\mu}\omega_{\rho\nu} + \partial_{\nu}\omega_{\rho\mu}
\right) X_1^{\rho}X_2^{\mu}X_3^{\nu}.
\end{align}
++++
