== Coordinate transformation using differential forms

latexmath:[\mathbb{R}^2] 上での座標変換 latexmath:[(x, y) \mapsto (r, \theta)] を極座標変換とみなし、これがヤコビアンの計算と同様の関係を持つことを示す。

[stem,latexmath]
++++
\begin{aligned}
dx \wedge dy
&= \left(\frac{\partial x}{\partial r}dr + \frac{\partial x}{\partial \theta}d\theta \right) \wedge
\left(\frac{\partial y}{\partial r}dr + \frac{\partial y}{\partial \theta}d\theta \right) \\
&=
\frac{\partial x}{\partial r} \frac{\partial y}{\partial r} \, dr \wedge dr
+ \frac{\partial x}{\partial r} \frac{\partial y}{\partial \theta} \, dr \wedge d\theta
+ \frac{\partial x}{\partial \theta}\frac{\partial y}{\partial r} \, d\theta \wedge dr
+ \frac{\partial x}{\partial \theta} \frac{\partial y}{\partial \theta}\, d\theta \wedge d\theta \\
&=
\frac{\partial x}{\partial r} \frac{\partial y}{\partial \theta} \, dr \wedge d\theta
+ \frac{\partial x}{\partial \theta}\frac{\partial y}{\partial r} \, d\theta \wedge dr \\
&=
\left(
    \frac{\partial x}{\partial r} \frac{\partial y}{\partial \theta}
  - \frac{\partial x}{\partial \theta}\frac{\partial y}{\partial r}
\right) \, dr \wedge d\theta \\
&=
\det\begin{pmatrix}
  \frac{\partial x}{\partial r} & \frac{\partial x}{\partial \theta} \\
  \frac{\partial y}{\partial r} & \frac{\partial y}{\partial \theta} \\
\end{pmatrix} \, dr \wedge d\theta \\
\end{aligned}
++++

また、latexmath:[\mathbf{r}=(r\cos\theta, r\sin\theta)] として、極座標変換後の1-形式の係数を計算すると、次のようになる。

[stem,latexmath]
++++
\begin{aligned}
dx \wedge dy
&= d(r \cos\theta) \wedge d(r \sin\theta) \\
&= ((dr)\cos\theta - r \sin\theta\, d \theta) \wedge ((dr)\sin\theta + r \cos\theta\, d \theta) \\
&= r \cos^2 \theta \, d r \wedge d\theta - r \sin^2\theta \, d\theta \wedge dr \\
&= r \, dr \wedge d \theta \\
\end{aligned}
++++
